How to report a fault

Watch this short video, or read the steps below it.

{# preload="none" means the video is NOT downloaded until you press play, which saves mobile data. The browser uses the first source it can play. #}
  1. Tap Report a fault.
  2. Choose what kind of fault it is and type the street name.
  3. Describe the problem, then enter your name and cell number.
  4. Add a photo if you can, then tap Submit report.
  5. Write down your reference number, for example W42-0153.
  6. Come back later to Check status and type in that number.
